# Earnings Calendar Feature Launch — Strategy Brief

**For:** Thay
**Request:** Plan a launch campaign for Thay's new earnings calendar feature — strategic brief only, for copy/social/growth to build from afterward.

## Angle

Don't sell this as "a calendar." Every trading app already has a calendar tab — that's exactly the thing retail investors ignore because it means opening another app and scanning a wall of tickers that aren't theirs. The angle is **"stop checking, start getting told"**: Thay already knows which tickers you hold and watch, so it's the one that surfaces *your* earnings dates, unprompted, inside the chat you're already in. The feature isn't new information (earnings dates are public and everywhere) — it's removing the step where you have to go look.

Timing gives this a natural hook: as of launch (early August 2026), Q2 2026 earnings season is in its tail end — mega-caps mostly done, but plenty of the names retail investors actually hold are still reporting through August. Q3 season doesn't kick off until mid-October (banks first, then mega-cap tech in the back half of the month). That gives two usable beats rather than one launch spike: a "some of your names are still due to report this month" push now, and a second "Q3 season starts — here's your calendar" beat in mid-October. Content/social/growth can decide whether to use both or just the first.

## Audience segment

Primary: **existing Thay users who already have a watchlist or holdings tracked in-chat.** This is the segment the feature is actually built for — the value only clicks once Thay knows what you hold. Treat this as retention/engagement, not pure acquisition: the goal is "look what Thay already does for you" more than "come try Thay."

Secondary: **prospective users currently juggling a separate broker app calendar tab, a spreadsheet, or a finance Twitter/X list to track earnings on the specific names they trade.** This segment is a fair growth angle (the pain — "I found out AAPL reported after the fact" — is real and common) but the copy needs to stay in feature-description territory, not "you'll never miss a move again" territory (see compliance note below).

Don't conflate this with new-to-investing users — this feature assumes someone already has positions or a watchlist worth tracking.

## Content pillars

1. **"Your calendar, not the calendar"** — personalization is the whole point. Every message should imply "the earnings dates that matter to *you*," not a generic financial calendar.
2. **Never miss a call** — reliability/utility. Retail investors' real fear here is finding out after the fact that a stock they hold moved 8% overnight on an earnings beat/miss they didn't see coming.
3. **One less tab** — convenience/positioning. Reinforces Thay's existing "companion in the app you already have open" value prop (LINE) against apps that make you go elsewhere to check.
4. **Know what to watch for, not just when** — ties the calendar to Thay's existing analysis strength (this pillar is optional/secondary — use only if the feature actually surfaces context like consensus estimates or "why this print matters," not just a bare date. Flag to confirm with product before copy leans on it.)

## Key message

Thay already knows what you hold — now it tells you when those companies report, before you'd otherwise find out the hard way.

## Format suggestions

- **LINE broadcast** to existing users — primary vehicle, direct announcement, fits Thay's "knowledgeable friend" tone. Natural home for pillar 1 + 2.
- **Social post(s)** (X/Facebook, wherever Thay already posts) — a simple before/after framing ("checking 3 apps" vs. "Thay just tells you") works well as a short static or carousel. Good fit for pillar 3, and shareable enough to pull in the secondary acquisition segment.
- **Growth-owned**: consider a short-form ad or landing snippet targeting the "I missed an earnings move" pain point directly — growth-marketer should own whether this is paid social or organic-only, and needs to keep language in describe-the-feature territory per the compliance note below.
- Not recommending email or a dedicated landing page section for this one — it's a feature update for an existing chat product, not a new product; LINE + social carries it.

## Compliance flag for downstream specialists

Nothing here should imply the calendar itself creates trading opportunity or edge ("never miss a move" / "know before the market does" territory drifts toward outcome-promising). Keep copy anchored to what the feature *does* (surfaces earnings dates for tickers you hold/watch, inside chat) rather than what it *gets you* (better trades, avoided losses). Applies to LINE broadcast, social, and growth copy alike per the Thay brand brief's compliance guardrail.
