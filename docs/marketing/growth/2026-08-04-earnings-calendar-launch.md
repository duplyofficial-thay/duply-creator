# Earnings Calendar Launch — Growth Ideas

**For:** Thay
**Brief/angle:** "Stop checking, start getting told" — Thay already knows your holdings/watchlist, so it surfaces your earnings dates unprompted inside LINE chat, instead of you checking a separate tab. (Content strategist brief: `marketing/briefs/2026-08-04-earnings-calendar-launch.md`)

## Acquisition channels

The feature only works for people who already have a watchlist, so acquisition channels need to reach people who'll add tickers fast, not a broad finance audience.

- **Thai US-stock Facebook groups** (e.g. groups built around US stock trading, moomoo/Webull/Tiger user communities). This is where the secondary audience — "I found out AAPL reported after the fact" — already complains about exactly this problem. Seed with genuine comments and posts, not ad copy.
- **Pantip ห้องสินธร (Sinthorn)** — the standing home for Thai retail investor discussion, including US stocks. A post framed as a personal fix for a common annoyance fits this forum's norms better than promotional copy.
- **Thai fintwit (X)** — the finance commentary crowd that already discusses US earnings prints. A short before/after post here doubles as content for pillar 3 ("one less tab") and can be run organically without paid spend.
- **LINE OpenChat groups for US stock traders** — direct fit, since Thay lives in LINE already. Traders in these groups are one tap from adding Thay as a friend, which shortens the funnel considerably compared to any channel that dead-ends outside LINE.
- **Thai finance YouTube/TikTok creators who cover US stocks** — worth a light-touch seed (send the feature, not a brief) to two or three creators who already cover Thay or similar tools. Treat as earned, not a paid placement, for this launch.

Not recommending broad-reach paid display or search for this launch — the audience is narrow (people who already hold or watch specific US tickers) and channel-agnostic reach would spend against people who can't get value from the feature yet.

## Ad angles to test

Headline + targeting pairs for the copywriter to pick from — not final copy. All headlines describe what the feature does; none imply it prevents losses or creates trading edge, per the brief's compliance flag.

1. **"Found out AAPL reported after the move already happened?"** — targets Thai retail traders active in US-stock trading Facebook groups and forums, broker-app interest (Webull, moomoo, Tiger). Names the pain directly as a question, doesn't promise it won't happen again.
2. **"Still tracking earnings dates in a spreadsheet?"** — targets people with productivity-tool interest (Notion, Google Sheets) layered with stock trading interest. Goes after the specific "juggling a spreadsheet" behavior the brief calls out.
3. **"Your watchlist, minus the extra tab."** — LINE-native framing, ties to pillar 3. Best suited to organic social/LINE placement rather than cold paid targeting, since it assumes some familiarity with Thay already.
4. **"Thay already knows what you hold. Now it tells you when they report."** — closest to the brief's key message, works as a retargeting angle for people who've visited Thay's LINE OA or website but haven't added a ticker yet.

Angles 1 and 2 are the ones built for the secondary (prospective-user) audience and are the candidates for paid testing below. Angles 3 and 4 lean retention/existing-user and are better handled by copywriter/social as LINE broadcast and social post per the brief.

## Funnel / CTA suggestions

- **First touch (cold, secondary audience):** CTA is "Add Thay on LINE" — not "sign up" or "download," since the whole product lives in LINE. Landing point should be the LINE OA add link directly, skipping any intermediate page.
- **Try it (new LINE friend):** first-session nudge to name one ticker they hold or watch. This is the activation event — the earnings calendar feature has zero value until Thay has at least one ticker to track, so the funnel's real conversion goal is "added a ticker," not "added Thay."
- **Stick around (existing users, low watchlist activity):** a one-time in-chat nudge to complete the watchlist ("add the rest of what you hold") tied to the calendar feature specifically — "add your other tickers and Thay covers those earnings dates too." This is a retention lever more than acquisition, but it's the step that makes the whole funnel pay off, so flagging it here even though content-strategist/copy likely owns the actual message.
- **Share loop (optional, low-cost):** the before/after "checking 3 apps vs. Thay just tells you" social creative (pillar 3) is inherently shareable. Add an explicit forward prompt in the LINE broadcast version — "know someone who's still checking five tabs" — since peer referral inside existing trading friend groups is a plausible low-cost channel for a niche tool like this.

## Growth experiments

1. **Organic community seeding.** If we post the "I missed an earnings move" pain point natively in three Thai US-stock Facebook groups and on Pantip (not as an ad, as a genuine post/comment), we expect a measurable bump in LINE OA adds attributable to referral traffic from those threads within 48 hours, because retail traders in these communities trust peer-shared tools more than ads and the pain point is one they've likely posted about themselves.
2. **Landing/signup snippet test.** If we add a short earnings-calendar callout module to Thay's existing sign-up touchpoint (not a new page — a section, per the brief's note against a dedicated landing page) with the CTA "Add Thay, get told when your stocks report," we expect a lift in LINE-add conversion versus the current baseline, because it gives prospective users a concrete reason to act instead of an abstract positioning line.
3. **Small paid social test, contingent on (1).** If organic seeding in experiment 1 shows positive engagement (comments, shares, or a traceable bump in adds), we'll run a small-budget Facebook/Instagram test (suggest starting around 5,000–10,000 THB) using ad angle 1 or 2 targeted at Thai retail trader interest, and measure cost per LINE OA add. Running this only after organic validation, because there's no historical CAC data in this repo to size a blind paid test against, and financial-product ad policies on Meta carry review overhead that's worth avoiding until the angle is proven.
4. **Watchlist-completion nudge for existing users.** If Thay sends a one-time nudge to existing users with an empty or partial watchlist — "add your holdings, get their earnings dates too" — we expect an increase in watchlist completion rate, because the calendar feature's value is entirely gated behind having tickers tracked, and this experiment converts feature awareness into feature usage for the exact audience the brief calls primary.
5. **Referral forward test.** If the LINE broadcast version of the before/after social creative includes an explicit "forward to a friend who trades" line, we expect the forward rate to correlate with new LINE adds tagged to that broadcast, because it tests whether peer referral inside trading friend groups is a viable low-cost channel worth building into future launches.

**On paid vs. organic (per the brief, growth owns this call):** starting organic-only for this launch, with paid social as a contingent follow-on (experiment 3), not a day-one spend. Three reasons: there's no historical CAC or conversion data in this repo to size a paid test responsibly; the audience that gets value from this feature is narrow (people who already hold or watch specific tickers), so organic community channels reach a higher-intent slice than broad paid targeting would; and financial-product ads face extra platform review on Meta/Google, which adds risk and delay for a feature-update campaign that the brief already scopes as secondary to the retention push.
