## Direction

**Tawan helps Thai retail teams selling physical items by the piece respond consistently on LINE, capture customer and Order details like a sales administrator, and act on clear daily follow-ups—while authorized humans retain approval over money and exceptions.**

The initial pilot target is Thai small-to-medium social-commerce retailers and solo entrepreneurs that use, or are ready to use, their own LINE Official Account. Fashion and accessories remain useful representative test scenarios, but are not a restriction on pilot recruitment.

## Roadmap

| Phase | Outcome | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | A LINE-first, assisted pilot: onboarding, merchant-owned LINE OA, Store Brain, safe customer sales/admin support, action-first dashboard, and daily digest. | Tawan Official manages discovery, onboarding, support, and later merchant billing; each merchant's own LINE OA is customer-facing. |
| Phase 2 | Demand-proven integrations, owner-approved Pro campaigns, segmentation, and advanced insights. | Choose integrations from measured pilot demand; do not pre-commit to individual connectors. |
| Phase 3 | Mature multichannel operations, recurring billing, advanced intelligence, and optional merchant showcase/brand-ambassador work. | Any showcase requires a separate merchant opt-in commercial agreement. |

## Plan and payment boundary

| Offering | Agreed boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Pilot | Free for 30 days. Plan for ten merchants, but reassess the cohort size if recruitment evidence requires it. |
| Standard | The future paid core service: LINE sales/admin support, Store Brain, dashboard, and daily digest. Continuing merchants receive a Standard payment link through Tawan Official only after the pilot. Pricing is intentionally not set until pilot feedback and purchasing-power research are reviewed. |
| Pro | A future higher-paid tier for special, advanced capabilities. Its exact features and price require separate product-owner approval after pilot evidence; it is not a Phase 1 promise. Owner-approved campaign capabilities, segmentation, and advanced intelligence are candidates, not a finalized entitlement list. |

Tawan Official handles only **Tawan merchant subscription billing**. A customer's retail Order payment remains between that customer and the merchant's Store Workspace. In Phase 1, Tawan may prepare payment-review work, but only the Store Owner can make the final paid/rejected decision; Tawan cannot autonomously approve payment, refunds, discounts, cancellations, or exceptional prices.

## Pilot learning and success measures

The team surveys every pilot merchant at onboarding, around day 15, and at day 30. Before setting a post-pilot price or finalizing Pro, the product owner reviews qualitative feedback, willingness to pay and purchasing power, workload reduction, and usage evidence.

The current validation targets are:

1. Ten pilot merchants (a planning target, subject to recruitment evidence).
2. At least 80% onboarding completion.
3. Fourteen consecutive useful daily summaries for every active merchant.
4. At least 85% of routine replies need no correction.
5. At least two owner/staff hours saved per merchant each week.
6. Zero cross-store data leakage and zero unapproved commercial action.
7. At least 50% of active pilots convert to paid Standard. This is a validation target, not a commercial promise, because pricing follows the pilot survey and review.

## Explicit Phase 1 exclusions

- A public marketplace or shared customer-facing Tawan Official identity.
- Direct marketplace, POS, social, or multichannel connectors, including Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Facebook, Instagram, and Discord.
- Pro campaigns, proactive outreach, segmentation, and advanced customer intelligence.
- Autonomous payment completion, refunds, cancellations, discounts, price changes, or commercial exceptions.
- Identifiable cross-store Customer Memory, profiling, analytics, or model training.
- Real customer data before Thai counsel, security, runtime, tenant-isolation, and launch-gate evidence is complete.
- Detailed engineering architecture or implementation work; those are governed by later feature cards and verified platform contracts.
